Guide

How it works

  1. Resolve: typed name, link (page title) or photo (vision AI) becomes a product name.
  2. Search: DuckDuckGo, Bing and Open Food Facts are queried together; failures are shown, never hidden.
  3. Enrich: top pages are opened for image and price.
  4. Score: word overlap, character overlap, model/spec match and price. Spec conflicts (256GB vs 128GB) and variants (Pro, Max) cut the score.
  5. Verify: borderline pairs are judged by a free AI model.

Forward deployed engineer playbook

  • Check if a customer's new SKU already exists in their catalogue (upload CSV).
  • Find marketplace listings of a product from a link, for price monitoring.
  • Identify an unlabelled item from a photo, then find twins.
  • Export the report for the customer. Bulk clean-up: use Catalogue Lab.

Good to know

  • Twin 70%+, Likely 50-70%, Different below 50%.
  • Prices are shown in their own currency; the spread uses the most common one.
  • Search pages may rate-limit; try again or add a CSV.
  • Do not upload private photos or data to free AI tiers.

    Glossary

    Product offer
    One listing of a product on one website.
    Blocking
    Only comparing listings that share a rare word, so we skip millions of pointless comparisons.
    Fuzzy similarity
    A score from 0 to 1 instead of a yes/no, built from word overlap, character overlap, model-number match and price closeness.
    Jaccard
    Shared words divided by all words.
    Trigram Dice
    Overlap of 3-letter chunks. Survives typos.
    Genetic algorithm (GA)
    Tries many weight sets, keeps the best, mixes and mutates them.
    Precision
    Of the pairs we merged, how many were truly the same.
    Recall
    Of the truly same pairs, how many we found.
    Union-find
    A fast way to join matched pairs into clusters.
    Price spread
    Highest price divided by lowest within one cluster.
