    Glossary

    Product offer
    One listing of a product on one website.
    Blocking
    Only comparing listings that share a rare word, so we skip millions of pointless comparisons.
    Fuzzy similarity
    A score from 0 to 1 instead of a yes/no, built from word overlap, character overlap, model-number match and price closeness.
    Jaccard
    Shared words divided by all words.
    Trigram Dice
    Overlap of 3-letter chunks. Survives typos.
    Genetic algorithm (GA)
    Tries many weight sets, keeps the best, mixes and mutates them.
    Precision
    Of the pairs we merged, how many were truly the same.
    Recall
    Of the truly same pairs, how many we found.
    Union-find
    A fast way to join matched pairs into clusters.
    Price spread
    Highest price divided by lowest within one cluster.

    How to use, and what powers it

    1. Open Upload and run, load your CSV or the sample.
    2. Press Find duplicates. Rules work with no keys.
    3. If you have a label column, press Learn rules (GA) and compare F1.
    4. Check Clusters and Business view; download results.
    5. Open Doubtful pairs and ask AI only where needed.
    6. Work through the Checklist before handover.
    Used in this project
    • Vanilla JS in the browser: parsing, blocking, fuzzy scoring, GA, union-find
    • Vercel serverless function /api/explain for LLM fallback
    • Gemini free tier, Pollinations (no key), Ollama (self-hosted), template
    • Vercel static hosting. No paid API anywhere.

    Limits: stay under 4.5 MB per upload on the cloud; large corpora should be sliced first. Check the corpus licence before redistributing data.
